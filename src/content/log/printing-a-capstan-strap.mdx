Capstan drives normally use steel cable. Mine started from the open-source Capstan-Drive design, but replaces the cable with a flat strap, 3D-printed and anchored at both ends with an adjustable tensioner.

## Material

TPU was the first attempt, and I discarded it: too elastic for an axis that has to hold an angle. PETG at 1.0 mm is what's on the machine now.

## Why a custom generator

A slicer isn't built to lay down a strap as one continuous line. So I wrote a small Python tool with a PySide6 GUI that calculates extrusion and flow, generates the G-code and sends it directly to the Bambu Lab A1 over the network.
