### Board
**4-layer PCB** in KiCad, fabricated by JLCPCB and **hand-soldered**, including 0402 passives and QFN packages.

### Connectivity
**ESP32-S3** with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. A phone app handles settings and remote control, and lets the flash work with the phone's camera.

### Dongle
A small dongle on the camera triggers the flash remotely.
