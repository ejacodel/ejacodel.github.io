### Pumps
Two self-designed, self-made **3D-printed peristaltic pumps**, each driven by a NEMA 17 stepper.

### Closed loop
A **load cell and HX711** measure the dispensed mass, closing the loop on what actually arrived.

### Interface
An **ESP32** controller, with an OLED screen to choose recipes.
